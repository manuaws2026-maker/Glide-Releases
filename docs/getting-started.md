# Getting started

Glide takes a screen recording and turns it into a polished, share‑ready video. Here’s the whole flow.

## 1. Record (or open)

- Click **● Record** in the top bar to capture your screen. You can include your **camera** and **microphone**, choose the full display, a single window, or an area, and add a countdown.
- Already have a recording open from a previous session? Use **🗂 Open** to reopen any Glide project.

When recording stops, Glide automatically creates a project and starts auto‑saving, so your work is never lost.

## 2. Let Glide polish it

The moment your recording loads, Glide applies its signature polish:

- **Auto‑zoom** punches in on each click and eases back out.
- **Smooth cursor** replaces your real pointer with a weighted, gliding one.
- **A framed background** wraps the recording with padding, rounded corners, and a shadow.

Press **▶︎** to preview. It already looks like a real product demo — and you haven’t touched a setting yet.

## 3. Make it yours

Use the panel rail on the right to fine‑tune anything:

| Panel | What it controls |
|------|------------------|
| **Background** | Wallpaper / gradient / color, padding, corner radius, shadow, inset border |
| **Cursor** | Smoothing, size, click effects, shake removal |
| **Zoom** | Auto‑zoom on/off, zoom level, zoom‑in speed, motion blur |
| **Camera** | Webcam size, shape, position, mirror |
| **Audio** | Mic / system levels, voice clean‑up, background music |
| **Captions** | Auto‑transcribed, editable captions |
| **Voiceover** | AI‑written and ‑spoken narration (or record your own) |

Below the preview, the **timeline** lets you trim, cut, change speed, and rearrange zoom / caption / voice blocks.

## 4. Add a voiceover (optional)

Open the **Voiceover** panel, write a one‑line description of your product in the brief box, and click **✦ Generate**. Glide watches the video, writes a script aimed at your audience, and lays the lines out to fit. Pick a voice and click **Voice all** to hear it. Edit any line and re‑voice just that line with the **⟳** button.

Prefer your own voice? Add a line and hit **🎤** to record it.

## 5. Export & share

Click **⬆ Export**, choose a format (**MP4**, **GIF**, or **WebM**) and quality, and Glide renders a sharp final video — ready for your landing page, docs, or socials.

### Saving your project
- **💾 Save** keeps your edits in place (and confirms with **Saved ✓**).
- The **▾** next to Save offers **Save a copy…**, where you can name a fresh copy.
