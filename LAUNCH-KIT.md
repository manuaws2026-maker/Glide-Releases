**Founder first comment:**
Hi PH 👋

I build demos and tutorials constantly, and the recording was never the hard part — the voiceover was. Writing a tight script, re-recording the line I fumbled, normalizing audio, doing it again when the UI changed. I'd ship a clean recording with no narration just to avoid hearing my own voice for the tenth time.

So I built Glide. You record your screen, and Glide watches the footage, writes a script aimed at the audience you pick, and speaks it — in a clean AI voice or a clone of your own. If you want a presenter on screen, it can add an AI avatar. The polish layer is there too: auto-zoom that tracks your clicks, smoothed cursor paths, backgrounds, auto captions, and MP4/GIF export.

A few honest notes: it's a native Mac app for Apple Silicon, it's a free direct download, and you bring your own AI keys so nothing routes through a server I control. No account required.

What I'd love feedback on: does the auto-written script actually match how you'd narrate your own product? Tell me where it gets the tone wrong — that's the part I most want to sharpen. Thanks for taking a look.
